## Before using any of these on the site

- These came off a personal profile carrying business branding, not a Facebook Page, and the profile owner's own recent post says several high-end clients don't permit posting their spaces to Facebook at all. That post doesn't confirm one way or the other whether *this* boardroom client is fine with the photo moving from a personal FB timeline onto a commercial catalog site. Worth a quick check with Dad before publishing.
- Photo 01 has a faint reflected photographer silhouette in the glass panel on the far wall. Minor, but worth knowing before it goes on a Swiss-precision, high-polish site.
- None of these show the current product line's flagship items (Binary Abfuser, Diffuser, Absorption Panel) specifically. This boardroom's dark panel wall looks like a different, smoother acoustic panel finish, not the pyramid-slat "Acoustic Diffuser" or the perforated "Binary Abfuser." Good as general "real work we've done" proof, not as a stand-in product photo for any one of the three catalog items.
